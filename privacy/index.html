Nap Nap · Baby sleep, feed & diaper log

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 November 2026 · Developer TeaDesign

In short

  • All data stays on your device. Everything you log is stored only in the app's local storage on your phone or tablet.
  • The app collects nothing. We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any personal data or any data about your child.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. The app only checks for app updates when online (see section 3).

Nap Nap ("the app") is an offline-first app for logging a baby's sleep, feeds, diapers and other care activities. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

1Information you enter

You can enter information such as a child's name, date of birth, due date, sleep times, feeding and diaper logs, growth measurements, temperature, medicine, milestones and app settings. This information is saved only in a local database on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and we have no way to access it.

2Information we collect automatically

None. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking software, and it does not use device identifiers, location, contacts, camera or microphone.

3Network use

The app does not need an internet connection and never sends your logs or your child's details over the network.

When the phone is online, the app may check for a newer version of its own code using Expo's update service (Expo, operated by 650 Industries, Inc.), so that fixes reach you without a full store update. This check sends only technical information needed to deliver the update, such as the app version, the platform (iOS or Android) and your device's IP address as part of the normal internet connection. It contains nothing you entered in the app. Expo handles this under its own privacy policy (expo.dev/privacy).

The app stores (Apple App Store, Google Play) handle downloads and store updates under their own privacy policies.

4Feedback emails

If you tap "Send feedback", the app opens your own email app with a pre-filled message containing the app version and your phone's operating system version. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email. If you do, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply to you.

5Accounts

No account or sign-up is required or offered.

6Backups

If you have turned on device backups yourself (for example iCloud Backup or Google device backup), your phone's operating system may include the app's local data in those backups. Those backups are controlled by you and by Apple or Google under their own terms, not by us.

7Deleting your data

You can delete a child and all of that child's logs inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.

8Children's privacy

The app is meant for parents and caregivers. Information about a child is entered by the parent or caregiver and remains only on their device. Because the app collects no data, we collect no personal information from or about children (including under COPPA and the GDPR).

9Medical disclaimer

Sleep predictions, schedules and insights in the app are informational parenting aids based on typical age ranges and your own logs. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow safe-sleep guidance and talk to a paediatrician or other qualified health professional about any concern with your child's sleep, feeding, growth or health. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

10Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this address. If a future version of the app adds any optional feature that handles data differently, it will be described here before release.
